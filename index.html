Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about company secretary services in India.

What is the role of a Company Secretary in India?

+
A Company Secretary in India serves as key managerial personnel responsible for ensuring compliance with corporate laws, maintaining statutory records, facilitating board meetings, and acting as a liaison between the company and regulatory authorities — MCA, SEBI, and ROC.

How can MSA Professional help my business?

+
MSA Professional provides end-to-end company secretarial services: company formation, statutory compliance management, corporate governance advisory, FEMA/FDI compliance, corporate restructuring, and legal due diligence — all delivered personally by FCS Mritunjay Chandra Shekhar (ICSI No. FCS 12594).

Is it mandatory to appoint a Company Secretary?

+
Under the Companies Act, 2013, every listed company and certain unlisted public companies (paid-up capital ₹10 crore or more) must appoint a whole-time Company Secretary as Key Managerial Personnel. Even companies not legally required to appoint one benefit greatly from professional CS services.

Do you assist with company formation outside India?

+
Yes. MSA Professional has extensive experience in cross-border company formation across 12+ countries including Dubai, Singapore, Hong Kong, USA, Netherlands, Malaysia, and several African nations. We handle end-to-end incorporation, bank account opening, and all regulatory filings.
